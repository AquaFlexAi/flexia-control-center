# Ethical Principles & Shariah Framework

## 1. Axioms of Justice
The FlexIA ecosystem is built upon the rejection of exploitative economic models. We align our decentralized architecture with **Islamic Finance (Maqasid al-Shariah)** to ensure that technology serves humanity without the corruption of gambling or excessive risk.

## 2. Rejection of Speculation (Gharar & Maysir)
Traditional "Staking" models in Web3 often resemble speculative gambling. FlexIA replaces this with:
- **Productive Asset Backing**: The network's value is derived from real-time GPU inference and bandwidth capacity, not simulated scarcity.
- **Mudarabah (Profit-Sharing)**: Investors (Rabb-ul-Mal) and Operators (Mudarib) share the actual profits generated by the network’s services.

## 3. The Wage vs. Profit Distinction (Ujrah)
We separate the cost of labor from the distribution of wealth:
- **Ujrah (Wages)**: Miners receive fixed-utility rewards (FLA) for the specific work of inference, ensuring their operational costs are covered regardless of token price movements.
- **Profit (Dividends)**: Surplus revenue from the network is distributed to the **Sovereign Profit Pool** for long-term stakeholders.

## 4. Transparency & Honest Management
As a decentralized protocol, transparency is not an option; it is an on-chain requirement.
- **Open Protocols**: All routing and settlement logic is open-source and verifiable.
- **Honest Attribution**: Hardware Attestation ensures that miners are honest about their capabilities, preventing deception (Gharar) in the marketplace.

## 5. Universal Access & Sovereignty
The network remains **Open-Permissionless**. No single entity can block access to the network's resources, ensuring that AI remains a "Public Utility" rather than a private weapon.
