# Global Implementation Strategy

## 1. The Evolutionary Thesis
The FlexIA Sovereign Network is designed to transition from a managed, incentive-aligned alpha phase to a fully autonomous, self-healing decentralized AI ecosystem. This roadmap outlines the cryptographic and economic milestones required to reach "Sovereign Ubiquity."

## 2. Phase Taxonomy

### Phase 1-2: Foundation & Connectivity (Completed)
- **Objective**: Establish the P2P transport layer and unified control plane.
- **Key Milestones**: libp2p integration, mTLS secure proxying, and the creation of the **Consensus Layer** anchors.

### Phase 4-5: Autonomous Settlement & Reputation (Completed)
- **Objective**: Activate the Mudarabah-based economic engine and decentralized auditing.
- **Key Milestones**: Automated voucher submission, UUEF probing, and on-chain reputation management.

### Phase 6: Sovereign Multidimensional Chain (SMC) (Completed)
- **Objective**: Implement hardware-aware resource isolation and secure discovery.
- **Key Milestones**: Hardware Attestation (SGX), On-Chain Capability Registry, and Verified Dimensional Handshake.

### Phase 7: Recursive Dimensional Settlement (Upcoming)
- **Objective**: Enable multi-hop dimensional liquidity and complex cross-chain settlement.
- **Key Milestones**: Inter-dimensional credit swaps, recursive attestation chains, and automated resource mediation.

## 4. Architectural Guardrails
Every phase is governed by three fundamental principles:
- **Productive Asset Backing**: Rewards must always be tied to verifiable work.
- **Privacy First**: User traffic must remain unlinkable to financial identity.
- **Shariah Integrity**: Rejection of speculative gambling in favor of partnership-based growth.
