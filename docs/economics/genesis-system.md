# The Genesis System (Phase 0)

## 1. The Bootstrapping Thesis
FlexIA is an asset-backed network. Before the public exchange of FLX/FLA tokens, the protocol must secure a "Genesis Supply" of verified compute capacity. Phase 0 is the dedicated period for early GPU/CPU contributors to prove their hardware capacity.

## 2. Genesis Badges (Proof of Contribution)
Early participants who register and sustain a node during Phase 0 earn a **Genesis Badge**.
- **Nature**: Soulbound NTT (Non-Transferable Token). It represents the node's history and contribution rank.
- **Requirement**: Maintaining a >90% uptime during the alpha testing period and successfully passing UEF health probes.
- **Utility**:
  - **Contribution Points**: Tracked on-chain to determine future airdrop eligibility.
  - **Reputation Boost**: Permanent legacy reputation for the miner node.
  - **Access Priority**: Early access to high-yield AI Dimensions.

## 3. Alpha Incentives
During the alpha phase, rewards are distributed as **Contribution Points** rather than liquid tokens. This ensures that only those who actually help build the network are rewarded, filtering out speculators.

## 4. Transition to Phase 1
Once the network reaches a critical mass of verified Genesis Nodes, the **Sovereign Hyper-Hub** will be activated, allowing the conversion of contribution points into liquid FLX/FLA units. This transition ensures that the token launch is backed by a fully functional P2P mesh from day one.
