# Tokenomics: FLX & FLA

FlexIA utilizes a **Dual-Token Architecture** to separate the volatility of ecosystem governance from the stability required for enterprise AI compute.

## 1. FLX: The Sovereign Governance Token
FLX is the native asset of the FlexIA ecosystem, representing governance rights and profit-sharing eligibility.

### 💳 Hybrid Billing & Participation Tiers
FlexIA supports a hybrid participation model tailored for both traditional and crypto-native users.

| Tier | Tokens/Month | Requirement | Philosophy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Free** | 10k | 0 | Open access for validation. |
| **Pro** | 1M | 1,000 FLX Stake | Productive participation. |
| **Enterprise** | Unlimited | 10,000 FLX Stake | Strategic infrastructure alignment. |

- **Utility**: Governs protocol upgrades, manages dimensional entries via the Hyper-Hub.
- **Incentive**: Eligible for **Mudarabah Profits** when staked in the `SovereignProfitPool`.
- **Scarcity**: Deflationary mechanics integrated into the Service Hub (Burn-on-Swap).

## 2. FLA: The AI Compute Credit (Wage Token)
FLA is the internal accounting unit for the AI Dimension. It is optimized for high-velocity usage.
- **Utility**: Used by consumers to pay for AI Inference.
- **Wage Mechanism**: Paid to miners as **Ujrah** for compute work.
- **Pricing**: Pegged to the "Cost of Compute" via the protocol’s economic verifier.

## 3. The Lifecycle of Value
1. **Acquisition**: A user burns/swaps `FLX` or `SAI` to mint `FLA` credits for AI tasks.
2. **Execution**: The user pays the **Miner** in `FLA` for generating inference.
3. **Settlement**: 
   - 90% of the `FLA` is disbursed to the Miner as **Wages (Ujrah)**.
   - 10% is routed to the **SovereignRevenueHub** as protocol surplus.
4. **Profit Distribution**: The Revenue Hub routes surpluses to the **Profit Pool**, where it is distributed to long-term `FLX` holders as **Dividends**.

## 4. Dynamic Equilibrium
The protocol monitors the supply of FLA vs. the global GPU capacity. If the network is over-capacity, FLA rewards may adjust to incentivize further hardware expansion, ensuring the ecosystem is always asset-backed by physical compute.
