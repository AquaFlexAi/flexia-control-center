# Shariah Compliance Checklist

To maintain the ethical integrity of the FlexIA ecosystem, all participants are encouraged to follow this operational checklist.

## 1. For Developers (Protocol Enforcers)
- [ ] **No Hidden Interest**: Ensure that no reward distributions include debt-based interest (Riba).
- [ ] **Transparency**: Smart contract logic for profit-sharing (Mudarabah) must be fully verifiable.
- [ ] **Risk Disclosure**: Clearly communicate the technical risks to miners to avoid excessive uncertainty (Gharar).

## 2. For Node Operators (Miners/Mudaribs)
- [ ] **Honest Advertising**: Do not spoof GPU capabilities in the DHT records.
- [ ] **Quality of Service**: Maintain high uptime to ensure the users (consumers) receive the value they paid for.
- [ ] **Privacy Respect**: Follow the onion-routing protocols to protect the data sovereignty of the users.

## 3. For Stakeholders (Rabb-ul-Mal)
- [ ] **Informed Participation**: Understand that profits are tied to network productivity, not guaranteed "dividends."
- [ ] **Governance Engagement**: Use FLX voting rights to prevent the introduction of predatory economic mechanics.

## 4. Operational Prohibitions
- **No Maysir**: Avoid creating "lottery-style" reward systems where probability of reward is decoupled from work.
- **No Exploitation**: Ensure that the **Ujrah** (Wages) paid to miners are fair and sufficient to cover hardware costs.
- **No Deception**: Hardware Attestation should not be bypassed using simulated virtual resources.

By following these principles, we ensure that FlexIA remains a beacon of justice in the decentralized world.
